COMPANY EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY:
1. Daily Meal Allowance: Business travel meals are reimbursable up to a maximum of $60 per day. Itemized tax receipts are mandatory; non-itemized card charge slips are not accepted. Alcoholic beverages are strictly non-reimbursable.
2. Hotel Accommodation: Standard hotel room rate must not exceed $180 per night excluding municipal taxes. Any room rate exceeding this limit requires written pre-approval from the Department Head prior to travel.
3. Ground Transit & Ride-Sharing: Standard ride-shares (UberX, Lyft standard) are reimbursable for transit between airport/hotel and client sites. Premium categories (Uber Black, Uber XL unless accompanied by 3 or more colleagues) are non-reimbursable.
4. Submission Deadline: All expense claims must be filed within 30 days of the date of occurrence. Claims filed after 30 days will be rejected automatically.

COMPANY LEAVE POLICY:
1. Sick Leave: Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Any consecutive sick leave absence exceeding 3 business days requires an official medical fitness certificate signed by a registered medical practitioner.
2. Casual / Annual Leave: Annual vacation leave exceeding 4 consecutive business days must be requested at least 14 days in advance.
3. Bereavement Leave: Up to 5 consecutive paid business days granted for immediate family members.
